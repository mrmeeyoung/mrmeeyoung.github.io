Who is ORE?

ORE is the creator name of Oreoluwa Okikiade, a Lagos-based Afrobeats interpreter and music curator. He is known as Mr. Nostalgia and goes by the username Mrmeeyoung on TikTok, X and YouTube.

Who is Mr. Nostalgia?

Mr. Nostalgia is ORE (Oreoluwa Okikiade), the Lagos-based creator who brings back the Afrobeats songs that raised a generation. Fans and event audiences gave him the name.

Who is Mrmeeyoung?

Mrmeeyoung (also written Mr Meeyoung) is the username of ORE, Oreoluwa Okikiade, the Afrobeats interpreter and music curator known as Mr. Nostalgia. He posts on TikTok as @mrmeeyoung.

What content does ORE make?

Each TikTok post pairs one classic Afrobeats song with one selfie and a few written lines about the song's meaning. No skits, no dancing: the song does the talking.

Since July 2025, the page has grown to 32.7K followers and more than 2.5 million likes, all organic (TikTok Analytics, 21 November 2026). His most-viewed post, on Sugarboy's "Hola Hola", has passed 615,000 views.

Is ORE a music curator?

Yes. ORE curates two Spotify playlists, Dear ORE, Play That Again and Dear ORE, Play That Again. S2, with 1,162 saves combined. They place songs from rising artists beside the classics.

Where is ORE based?

ORE is based in Lagos, Nigeria. He is also co-founder of SmileFestNG, a Lagos event for Gen Z built around music from every era, and convener of WSOTS Foundation.

How can brands work with ORE?

ORE is open to brand partnerships, artist and song campaigns, Spotify and Apple Music campaigns, voice-over work, brand events, podcasts, interviews and music panels.
