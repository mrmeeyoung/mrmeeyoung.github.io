01 About

ORE, Afrobeats interpreter

Oreoluwa Gift Okikiade (ORE), known online as Mr Meeyoung, is a Lagos-based Afrobeats interpreter and music curator.

Since July 2025, ORE has been bringing back the Afrobeats songs that raised a generation on TikTok, as @mrmeeyoung. Each post pairs one classic song with one selfie and a few written lines that give the record its meaning back. No skits, no dancing: the song does the talking.

As of 21 November 2026, the page had 32.7K followers and more than 2.5 million likes, all organic. Fans and event audiences call him Mr. Nostalgia.

05 Beyond the feed

Off the screen

ORE is co-founder of SmileFestNG, a Lagos event and community platform for Gen Z built around music from every era, held every December.

He is also the convener of WSOTS Foundation (Warmhearted Spacers on the Street Empowerment Foundation), which renovates schools and supports clean-water projects in Nigeria.

He studied Business Administration.
